2

Render paths — who draws?

local draw vs OverlayClient.annotate_result("main", result): the platform draws on the console's main stream — zero pixels leave your app.

overlay:

every annotate carries ttl_ms=1000 — boxes self-expire if the app stops publishing. Watch the console's main player while toggling.

Hardware routing

set_route_policy() steers JPEG/draw ops between NPU/DSP/CPU; deltas below are per-op counters since the last policy switch.

hardware_only never silently falls back — a refused op raises. That's the refusal demo: routing errors are loud, not swallowed.

4

A form — StreamPipeline

the platform subscribes, infers and draws on third; the app only consumes results: StreamPipeline("third", model, fps=10).

geometry rule: platform-side feeding has no scaler, so the stream must equal the model input — third (640×384) works, main 1080p rejects every frame with DMA -2811.
